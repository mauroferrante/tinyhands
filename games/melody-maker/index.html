Melody Maker

Play the piano and learn melodies!

▶ Play Melody Maker

Free · No ads · No account · Phone, tablet and laptop

What it is

Melody Maker is an eight-key piano with three ways to play. Free Play is just the keys: tap or press A to K and hear a warm piano note. Learn Melodies is a lesson mode where a friendly lion plays a short tune while a bouncing ball shows which key is next, then it is your turn to play it back. Thirty melodies build from three notes to real songs like Twinkle Twinkle, Happy Birthday and Ode to Joy. Song Parade is a play-along mode where notes fall toward the keyboard and a whale conducts. Progress is saved so children can pick up where they left off.

How to play

  • On a laptop or desktop: Keys A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K play Do to high Do. Space or Enter continue after a completed melody.
  • On a tablet or phone: Tap the colored keys. In lessons, watch the ball, then tap the keys in the same order. A replay button repeats the demo; a turtle button slows it down.

What children learn

  • Pitch discrimination and ear training
  • Sequential memory: holding a pattern of notes
  • Rhythm, with feedback on steady timing
  • Confidence from playing recognizable songs

Tips for parents

Start in Free Play so the piano feels like a toy before it feels like a lesson. In Learn Melodies, the slow-down button is there for a reason; a four-year-old will need it on longer tunes. Phones work best in landscape.

Questions parents ask

Which songs are in it?
Thirty melodies including Hot Cross Buns, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Old MacDonald, Row Your Boat, London Bridge, Jingle Bells, Twinkle Twinkle, Happy Birthday and Ode to Joy, plus short exercises that teach intervals first.
Does it need a real piano keyboard?
No. The eight on-screen keys work with taps, and a computer keyboard maps A to K to the notes.
Is progress saved?
Yes, on the device. Completed melodies show a star, and the next one is highlighted.
