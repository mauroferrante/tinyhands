Dress-Up Party

Dress a furry friend in silly outfits!

▶ Play Dress-Up Party

Free · No ads · No account · Phone, tablet and laptop

What it is

Dress-Up Party is a creativity game built around one gesture a two-year-old already has: the tap. A furry friend stands in the middle of the screen. Tap its head for a new hat, its eyes for glasses, its tummy for a top, its feet for shoes and its paw for a toy. Tap the background to change the scene from park to beach to circus. Tap the face to swap the animal. When the outfit is ready, the camera button flashes, the friend dances, and a polaroid appears with a silly name like Royal Bunny or Sporty Frog. A dice button makes a surprise outfit for children who just want to see what happens.

How to play

  • On a laptop or desktop: Click parts of the friend to change items. Letter keys change a slot, Space is a surprise outfit, Enter takes the photo.
  • On a tablet or phone: Tap the friend, tap the background, tap the wardrobe strip. Everything is one tap.

What children learn

  • Creative choice: there is no right outfit
  • Clothing and color vocabulary in conversation
  • Cause and effect with a visible, immediate result
  • Fine motor aiming at large targets

Tips for parents

Ask questions instead of directing: "What should Bunny wear to the beach?" The photo card is a natural stopping point, and the Share button on it lets a parent send the photo to a grandparent.

Questions parents ask

Is there dragging?
No. Drag-and-drop is hard for two-year-olds, so every change is a tap. Tapping the same spot cycles through the options.
Can we save the photo?
The polaroid stays on screen until you tap Dress up again. Use your device's screenshot to keep it, or the Share button to send the site to another parent.
How many outfits are there?
Six friends, six hats, three pairs of glasses, seven tops, five pairs of shoes, eight toys and six scenes. Thousands of combinations.
