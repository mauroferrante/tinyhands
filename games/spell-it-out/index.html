Spell It Out

Find the missing letter!

▶ Play Spell It Out

Free · No ads · No account · Phone, tablet and laptop

What it is

Spell It Out shows a picture, an emoji cat for example, and its word with one letter missing: C_T. The child presses or taps the missing letter. A right answer fills the tile with a chime and sparkles; a wrong one shows the correct letter, costs a life, and moves on. Words run from three letters to longer ones, streaks earn bigger and bigger celebrations, and the game remembers a best score. It is deliberately gentle: three lives, plenty of encouragement, and the right letter is always shown after a miss.

How to play

  • On a laptop or desktop: Type the missing letter on the keyboard. Space or Enter restarts after a game.
  • On a tablet or phone: Tap the letter on the on-screen keyboard. Used letters gray out so the choice narrows.

What children learn

  • Letter recognition and letter-sound links
  • Early spelling of common nouns
  • Picture-to-word association
  • Persistence: streaks reward staying focused

Tips for parents

Say the word out loud slowly and let the child hear the missing sound before they look for the letter. If they are guessing, cover the picture and read the word together first.

Questions parents ask

Are the words in American or British spelling?
The word list uses spellings that are the same in both, like CAT, MOON and FROG, so nobody is marked wrong for a regional difference.
What happens when the lives run out?
A friendly end card shows the score and best score with a tap-to-restart. There is no penalty beyond starting a new round.
My child can't type yet. Does it still work?
Yes. On touch devices an on-screen keyboard appears, and on desktop a parent can point to the key.
