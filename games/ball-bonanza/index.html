Ball Bonanza

Tap to bounce the ball to your animal friends!

▶ Play Ball Bonanza

Free · No ads · No account · Phone, tablet and laptop

What it is

Ball Bonanza is a physics playground with no goal except delight. A ball bounces around the screen among a crowd of emoji animals. Tap anywhere and the ball flies toward your finger, knocking the animals into spins, squashes and surprised faces. Every so often a star, a clock or a circus tent appears; touching them with the ball adds a bonus ball, speeds everything up, or invites more friends. Nothing needs to be finished and nothing can be lost.

How to play

  • On a laptop or desktop: Press any key to kick the ball in a random direction. Click to kick it toward the pointer.
  • On a tablet or phone: Tap where you want the ball to go. New fingers kick, so a whole hand on the screen sends it flying.

What children learn

  • Cause and effect with a delay: the tap comes first, the bounce follows
  • Early spatial reasoning: aiming toward a target
  • Anticipation and tracking a moving object
  • Emotional vocabulary from the animals' reactions

Tips for parents

Talk about what the animals are doing: dizzy, surprised, wobbly. Two-year-olds love naming reactions. If the ball gets stuck in a corner, one tap frees it.

Questions parents ask

Is there a way to win?
No. Ball Bonanza is a sandbox. Children play until they are done, and nothing resets or fails.
Why do the animals change sometimes?
Every couple of minutes the animals wave goodbye and a new group walks in, so a long session stays fresh.
Does it work with a mouse?
Yes. Click to aim the ball, or press any key for a random kick.
