Rocket Ride

Blast off and dodge the asteroids!

What it is

Rocket Ride is the fastest game on Tiny Hands Play. A rocket launches from a countdown and climbs through space while asteroids, satellites, space junk and eventually UFOs drift into its path. Steer left and right, boost upward, brake to slow down, and collect stars and fuel cans that give a burst of invincibility. Altitude and stars both count, best results are saved, and the higher you go the faster it all comes. Milestone chimes mark every few hundred kilometers.

How to play

  • On a laptop or desktop: Arrow keys or WASD: left and right steer, up or Space boosts, down brakes. Any key launches.
  • On a tablet or phone: Touch the left or right side of the screen to steer, hold to boost. Two thumbs work well.

What children learn

  • Momentum: the rocket keeps moving after you stop pressing
  • Reaction time and planning ahead
  • Left-right directional control
  • Reading a rising number (altitude) as a goal

Tips for parents

Boosting the whole time is the beginner's mistake; short boosts and steering early are the skill. Talk about momentum: why does the rocket keep drifting after you let go?

Questions parents ask

What is the best age for Rocket Ride?
Five and up. It needs two-directional steering and some planning, which most four-year-olds find frustrating.
Does it use the phone's tilt sensor?
No. Steering is by touch zones on the left and right of the screen, which is more predictable for kids than tilting.
How do lives work?
Three lives. Hitting an asteroid costs one and gives a few seconds of invincibility to recover. Fuel cans also make you invincible for a moment.
